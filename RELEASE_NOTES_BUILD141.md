# Slate Studio — Build 141

## Realistic base-mesh foundation, phase 2
- Upper-arm tubes now begin slightly inside the torso and taper outward from the shoulder.
- Refined deltoid transition volumes to reduce the oversized spherical shoulder appearance.
- Extended thigh profiles upward into the hip volume for a softer hip-to-leg transition.
- Reduced elbow, wrist, hip and knee transition ellipsoids to make the joint forms less ball-like.
- Widened the skinned weight blend zone along limb tubes for smoother deformation across joints.
- Preserved existing skeleton names, animation hierarchy, materials and customization controls.

Limitations: torso and limb surfaces are still separate components and some overlap remains. No browser/iPad visual test was run; validate poses and body types in the app.
